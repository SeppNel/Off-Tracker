Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2025

No Data Collection

Pushy Penguins does not collect, store, or share any personal data. We value your privacy, and therefore, no information is collected when you use our app. There are no analytics, tracking, or third-party services integrated into the app.

If you decide to post your score in the online leaderboard, only the score and the user specified name will be sent to the server. Nothing more.

Changes to This Policy

If we decide to change our Privacy Policy, we will post those changes on this page. The date of the latest update will always be provided at the top of this page.
